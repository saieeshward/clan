# Investment Strategy for Irish Residents (€100 allocation examples)

## Overview

This document provides three risk-appropriate investment strategies for Irish residents, accounting for:
- Irish tax regulations (41% ETF exit tax, 33% CGT on stocks, 33% DIRT on savings)
- 8-year ETF deemed disposal rule
- €1,270 annual CGT exemption
- Available platforms with favorable tax treatment
- PRSA tax relief benefits

All allocations assume €100 initial investment for illustration. Scale proportionally for larger amounts.

---

## 1. CONSERVATIVE PROFILE (Low Risk, Capital Preservation)

**Target audience**: Risk-averse investors, near-term withdrawals, emergency funds

### Allocation Breakdown
| Vehicle | Product | Platform | € | % | Post-Tax Expected Annual Return |
|---------|---------|----------|---|---|------|
| DIRT-exempt savings | An Post Savings Account | An Post | 50 | 50% | ~3.0% (guaranteed, no tax) |
| Irish-domiciled ETF | IWDA (MSCI World) | DeGiro | 30 | 30% | ~5.5% (after 41% exit tax + deemed disposal) |
| Individual stocks | Diversified Irish equities (e.g., AIB, CRH) | Trading 212 | 15 | 15% | ~3.5% (after 33% CGT, using exemption) |
| Cash buffer | Current account | Bank of Ireland / AIB | 5 | 5% | ~0.5% (micro-interest) |

**Weighted Portfolio Expected Return**: ~3.8% annually (post-tax)

### Getting Started (3 Steps)
1. **Week 1**: Open An Post account (proof of address + ID), deposit €50. Verify €1,270 CGT exemption status in your tax records.
2. **Week 2**: Create DeGiro account, deposit €30, purchase 0.3 shares of IWDA (€100 per share ≈ Dec 2024 pricing). Enable dividend reinvestment.
3. **Week 3**: Create Trading 212 account, deposit €15, buy individual Irish stocks (€3 each: AIB, CRH, Ryanair, or similar in €5 lots). Document purchase dates for CGT tracking.

### Tax Efficiency Notes
- An Post: No DIRT (0% tax), no exit restrictions
- IWDA on DeGiro: Deferrable tax via 8-year deemed disposal; reinvest dividends
- Irish stocks: First €1,270 gains exempt annually; then 33% CGT

## 2. BALANCED PROFILE (Moderate Risk) — **RECOMMENDED FOR BEGINNERS**

**Target audience**: 5-10 year investment horizon, steady wealth accumulation, balanced risk/reward

### Allocation Breakdown
| Vehicle | Product | Platform | € | % | Post-Tax Expected Annual Return |
|---------|---------|----------|---|---|------|
| DIRT-exempt savings | An Post Savings Account | An Post | 20 | 20% | ~3.0% (no tax) |
| Irish-domiciled ETFs | IWDA (50%) + VWRL (50%) | DeGiro | 55 | 55% | ~6.2% (after 41% exit tax) |
| Individual stocks | Dividend-paying Irish/EU stocks | Trading 212 | 20 | 20% | ~4.8% (after 33% CGT, exemption) |
| PRSA (pension) | Diversified PRSA fund | Fidelity or Irish Life | 5 | 5% | ~7.5% (tax-relieved growth, deferred exit tax) |

**Weighted Portfolio Expected Return**: ~5.6% annually (post-tax and post-relief)

### Getting Started (3 Steps)
1. **Week 1**: Open An Post account (€20), open Fidelity/Irish Life PRSA account (€5, auto-enrolled relief at 20% marginal rate = €1 tax refund). Verify tax band.
2. **Week 2**: Create DeGiro account (€55). Purchase: €27.50 in IWDA (0.28 shares) and €27.50 in VWRL (0.15 shares). Set dividend reinvestment to "yes."
3. **Week 3**: Create Trading 212 account (€20). Buy 4-5 dividend stocks: mix of AIB (bank, ~3.5% yield), Smurfit Kappa (packaging, ~2.5%), Glenveagh (real estate, ~4%). Stagger purchases across weeks to average cost.

### Tax Efficiency Notes
- PRSA: Contributions get 20-40% tax relief immediately; no 41% exit tax until withdrawal (60+); pension fund growth is untaxed during accumulation
- IWDA vs VWRL: IWDA is IE-domiciled (lighter admin), VWRL offers broader geographic diversification; both hit 41% exit tax at deemed disposal
- Dividends on individual stocks: Reinvest to defer CGT; only tax gains (not reinvested dividends) when sold
- Trade-off: ETFs have tax drag (41% exit tax) but lower effort; stocks require €1,270 exemption tracking

### Getting Started (3 Steps)
1. **Week 1**: Create Fidelity PRSA account (€10); elect all-equity aggressive fund (100% global equities). Confirm 20% tax relief (~€2 refund).
2. **Week 2**: Create DeGiro account (€65). Purchase: IWDA €32.50 (0.33 shares), VWRL €26 (0.14 shares), CSPX €6.50 (0.015 shares, using ~€450 price). Enable full reinvestment of dividends.
3. **Week 3-4**: Create Trading 212 account (€25). Deploy capital across 5 positions: Kingspan (construction/materials), Unilever (dividend + growth), Paddy Power (betting/gaming volatility), CRH (building materials), Smurfit Kappa (EBITDA play). Commit to buy-and-hold for 10+ years; review annually.

### Tax Efficiency Notes
- Maximize PRSA: €3,000+/year contributions receive 20% relief (€600 free money); this accelerates long-term compounding
- ETF deemed disposal: At 8-year mark (2032 for 2024 purchase), expect 41% tax hit; plan for this via PRSA + stocks to balance
- Individual stocks: Commit to 10-year hold to minimize CGT events; first €1,270 annual gain is tax-free
- Avoid US-domiciled ETFs: PRIIPs restriction for retail investors; use IE-domiciled alternatives only (IWDA, VWRL, CSPX all IE-domiciled via Solactive/BlackRock)

## Post-Tax Return Calculations (Summary)

### Example: €100 IWDA (IE-domiciled ETF) over 8 years @ 7% gross CAGR

**Baseline (no tax)**: €100 → €185.99 over 8 years

**With 41% exit tax applied at year 8 (deemed disposal)**:
- Gain: €85.99
- Exit tax owed: €85.99 × 0.41 = €35.26
- Net proceeds: €100 + €85.99 - €35.26 = **€150.73**
- Effective CAGR after tax: **5.5%** (reduced from 7%)

### Example: €100 in Irish stocks (33% CGT, €1,270 exemption)

**Baseline**: €100 → €162 over 8 years @ 6% CAGR

**With CGT applied**:
- Gain: €62
- Taxable gain: €0 (within €1,270 annual exemption, or staggered across years)
- CGT paid: €0
- Net proceeds: **€162** (full tax benefit from exemption)
- Effective CAGR after tax: **6.0%** (no tax drag if under exemption)

## Rebalancing Strategy

### Annual Review (Recommended)
- **Conservative**: Review An Post rate vs alternatives (0.5h); rebalance if allocation drifts >10%
- **Balanced**: Rebalance if ETF allocation drifts >15% from 55% target; add new annual €1,270 stock gains before year-end
- **Growth**: Minimal rebalancing needed if PRSA + ETFs + stocks are segregated; check 8-year ETF deemed disposal date (2032 for 2024 purchase)

### Quarterly Dividend Reinvestment
- Set DeGiro to "reinvest dividends" automatically
- Check Trading 212 dividends monthly; reinvest manually if available or accrue for purchase
- PRSA dividends auto-reinvest (no action required)

### Tax Year Boundary (31 Dec / 5 Jan)
- Log all trades, gains/losses; calculate CGT exemption usage for upcoming year
- If nearing €1,270 limit, hold stock sales until next tax year or use ETFs (tax deferral via deemed disposal)
- Review PRSA contribution for next year (maximize relief)

## Disclaimer & Regulatory Note

**This is NOT financial advice.** This document:
- Reflects Irish tax law as of May 2026; consult a tax advisor for your personal situation
- Assumes no major law changes (e.g., ETF tax rates, deemed disposal rules)
- Does NOT constitute investment advice; market returns are not guaranteed
- Does NOT cover inheritance tax (CAT), pension death benefits, or spousal implications

**Before investing**:
1. Confirm your tax residency status with Revenue (www.revenue.ie)
2. Verify your CGT exemption tracking with your accountant
3. Check PRSA contribution limits based on income (2024: up to €3,000 for relief)
4. Assess your risk tolerance independently; loss scenarios are real
5. Ensure emergency fund exists separately (6 months expenses) before investing

**Platforms used**:
- An Post: Government-backed; no credit risk
- DeGiro: Custodian-held; segregated client funds
- Trading 212: FCA-regulated (UK); segregated client funds
- Fidelity PRSA: Irish-regulated; pension protection

**Key risks**:
- Market downturn: Portfolios can fall 25-50% in recessions
- Liquidity: ETFs liquid; stocks less so during market stress
- Tax changes: 41% ETF exit tax or CGT rates could increase
- Deemed disposal: Forced tax at 8-year mark on ETFs; plan ahead
