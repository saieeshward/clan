§ Taxation

The Irish Tax Landscape

Ireland applies different tax treatments depending on the vehicle — ETFs carry the heaviest burden with a unique 8-year deemed disposal mechanism.

Exchange Traded Funds
Highest Tax
41%
  • Exit tax on all gains & deemed gains
  • 8-year deemed disposal rule — tax triggered even without a sale
  • No annual exemption unlike CGT
  • Loss relief limited (cannot offset against CGT gains)
  • EU/Irish-domiciled ETFs only (PRIIPs blocks US ETFs)
Individual Stocks & Shares
CGT Rules
33%
  • Capital Gains Tax on disposal
  • €1,270 annual CGT exemption per individual
  • Losses can be offset against gains in same year
  • Bed & Breakfast rule: 4-week waiting period
  • US stocks accessible (IBKR, DeGiro, Trading 212)
Deposit & Savings
DIRT
33%
  • Deposit Interest Retention Tax deducted at source
  • An Post State Savings: DIRT-exempt, government-guaranteed
  • Credit Union dividends: DIRT applies
  • PRSI 4% on unearned income >€5,000 (not on DIRT)
  • No deemed disposal — pure cash, no market risk
Pension (PRSA / DC)
Best Relief
20–40%
  • Contributions receive marginal rate income tax relief
  • Higher-rate taxpayers get 40% relief immediately
  • Age-based contribution limits (15–40% of net relevant earnings)
  • Growth inside pension is tax-free
  • Drawdown taxed at income rates — plan exit strategy

§ Getting Started

Balanced Profile: Step-by-Step

For a new Irish investor starting with €100/month on the recommended balanced allocation — open these accounts in order of priority.

01
Open a PRSA (20% of monthly budget)
Contact Irish Life, Zurich, or Aviva for a standard PRSA. Contribute €20/month — if you're a higher-rate taxpayer, Revenue returns €8 immediately (40% relief). Select a low-cost global equity fund (0.5–1% TER). Do this first: tax relief is the best guaranteed return available.
02
Open DeGiro (ETFs: IWDA + VWRL)
Register at DeGiro (EU-regulated). Buy IWDA (iShares Core MSCI World, LSE) with 40% of budget and VWRL (Vanguard FTSE All-World, LSE/Euronext) with 20%. Use accumulating share class to defer reinvestment tax. Note: 8-year deemed disposal applies — set a calendar reminder.
03
Deposit to An Post (15% of budget)
Open an An Post savings account (state.ie) and contribute €15/month. Choose the Prize Bonds or 3-Year Savings Certificate for DIRT-exempt, government-guaranteed returns. No market risk — this is your capital preservation layer.
04
Revolut Savings Pot (5% emergency buffer)
Maintain €5 in a Revolut Savings pot earning ~4% AER (subject to change). DIRT applies to interest, but this is your liquid emergency layer. Keep 3–6 months of expenses accessible before increasing equity exposure.
05
Annual CGT Harvesting (stocks layer)
Once your portfolio grows, use the €1,270 annual CGT exemption. Each December: sell enough stock gains to realise exactly €1,270, then repurchase after 4 weeks (Bed & Breakfast rule). This resets your cost basis and avoids future tax — free money if done consistently.
06
8-Year Deemed Disposal: Plan Ahead
Revenue taxes ETF gains at year 8 even without a sale. Track your ETF purchase dates. Set a reminder 12 months before each deemed disposal event — you may need cash to pay the tax. Consider structuring purchases in annual tranches to smooth the obligation over time.
PRSI 2024 Update: Since January 2024, PRSI at 4% applies to unearned income (dividends, rental) above €5,000 per year. This does not apply to ETF exit tax gains — they are handled separately. Stock CGT gains above €5,000 may attract PRSI. Confirm your liability with a tax advisor as thresholds can interact.
