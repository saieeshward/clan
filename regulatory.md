# Irish Investment Regulatory Framework

## Overview
This document consolidates verified Irish regulatory requirements and tax rules for investment activities by Dublin residents and Irish taxpayers. Information reflects 2026 tax rules and regulations.

---

## Tax Rates & Thresholds (2026)

| Tax Type | Rate | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Exit Tax (Fund-based)** | 41% | Applies to Irish-domiciled funds and EU UCITS funds |
| **Deemed Disposal** | 41% | Automatic every 8 years (mandatory, no sale needed) |
| **Capital Gains Tax (CGT)** | 33% | Individual shares, cryptoassets, second properties, tangible assets |
| **CGT Annual Exemption** | €1,270 | Per person per calendar year |
| **Deposit Interest Retention Tax (DIRT)** | 33% | Bank/savings interest, bond interest |
| **Pension Contribution Relief** | 20–40% | Marginal tax rate (standard 20%, higher rate 40%) |
| **PRSI on Unearned Income** | 4% (Class K) | Applies to unearned income >€5,000 p.a.; does NOT apply to exit tax gains |
| **Self-Assessment Threshold** | €5,000 | Non-PAYE income requiring Form 11 filing |

## Investor Classification & Market Access (MiFID II)

### Retail vs. Professional Status
- **Retail Investors (Default)**: Irish residents are classified as retail investors under MiFID II
- **Professional Eligibility**: Available only if meeting specific criteria (asset/income thresholds); requires opt-in classification
- **Regulatory Impact**: Retail classification restricts access to certain investment products

### US ETF Restrictions for Irish Retail Investors
- Irish retail investors **cannot directly access US-domiciled ETFs** (e.g., Vanguard VOO listed on NASDAQ)
- **Reason**: UCITS directive (EU 2009/65/EC) limits retail exposure to US equities via directly purchased ETFs
- **Alternatives**:
  - Purchase Irish-domiciled or EU-domiciled ETFs holding US equities (iShares S&P 500 UCITS ETF, VUSA)
  - Subject to exit tax (41%) and deemed disposal rules
  - Apply for professional investor status (if eligible) to access non-UCITS alternatives

## Deemed Disposal Mechanics (8-Year Rule)

### Trigger & Calculation
- **Automatic Event**: Occurs every 8 years from acquisition OR from previous deemed disposal event
- **No Action Required**: Fund position does NOT need to be sold; tax applies regardless
- **Tax Calculation**:
  - Gain = (Current Market Value − Acquisition Cost)
  - Tax Owed = Gain × 41%
  - Applies to funds held for 8+ years continuously

### Cash Flow & Credit Offset
- **Payment Required**: Exit tax is due even without liquidating the holding
- **Timing**: Self-assessed via Form 11; due with annual tax return (31 October following tax year end)
- **Credit Carry-Forward**: Exit tax paid on deemed disposal can be credited against exit tax on subsequent actual disposal (avoids double taxation)
- **Example**: €10,000 fund purchased January 2018 → €4,000 gain by January 2026 → 41% tax (€1,640) due Jan 2026 even if held. Upon sale in 2027, exit tax calculation credits €1,640 already paid.

### Impact on Reinvestment
- Exit tax reduces available capital unless funded externally
- Reinvestment post-deemed disposal resets the 8-year clock from the deemed disposal date

## Capital Gains Tax (CGT) – Individual Shares & Crypto

### Filing Deadlines
- **Gains Realized Jan–Nov**: Tax return due **15 December** of gain year
- **Gains Realized December**: Tax return due **31 January** following year
- **Form Required**: CG1 (Capital Gains Summary) attached to Form 11
- **Self-Assessment Penalties**: 5% uplift if return late; interest at 0.0322% daily if tax unpaid

### Calculation & Relief
- **Basis**: Cost basis = purchase price + transaction costs
- **Exemptions**: 
  - Primary residence (principal private residence relief)
  - Tangible moveable property <€3,000
  - Government securities (savings bonds, prize bonds)
- **Claim**: CGT exemption and reliefs claimed via Form 11

## Income Thresholds & Filing Requirements

### Self-Assessment Trigger (Form 11)
- **Threshold**: €5,000+ non-PAYE income in a calendar year triggers mandatory filing
- **Applicable Income**:
  - Investment gains (capital gains, interest, dividends)
  - Self-employment income
  - Rental income
  - Professional fees, authorship royalties
- **Exclusions**: Dividend income does NOT trigger filing if <€5,000 AND all DIRT/dividend withholding settled

### Form 11 vs. Form 12
| Form | Purpose | Who Files | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Form 11** | Self-employment, investment income, reliefs claimed | Self-employed, investors, high earners | 31 Oct following tax year |
| **Form 12** | PAYE employees claiming additional relief | Employees only | 15 Apr following tax year |

## Pension Contributions & Contribution Limits

### Relief Mechanism
- Contributions to Personal Retirement Savings Account (PRSA) receive tax relief at **marginal rate**:
  - 20% relief for standard-rate taxpayers
  - 40% relief for higher-rate taxpayers
- **Relief Claim**: Via Form 11 or employer payroll deduction (relief at source)

### Age-Based Annual Contribution Limits (2026)
- **Under 30**: No minimum; max €20,000/year (or 20% gross income, whichever is lower)
- **Age 30–39**: No minimum; max €23,000/year (or 25% gross income, whichever is lower)
- **Age 40–49**: No minimum; max €24,000/year (or 30% gross income, whichever is lower)
- **Age 50–55**: No minimum; max €28,000/year (or 35% gross income, whichever is lower)
- **Age 55+**: No minimum; max €32,000/year (or 40% gross income, whichever is lower)

### Non-Taxation of Pension Growth
- Investment returns within PRSA accumulate tax-free
- Exit tax and deemed disposal do NOT apply to PRSA holdings
- Significant advantage vs. taxable accounts

## PRSI 2024 Update (Class K – Unearned Income)

### Rule & Scope
- **Effective**: Tax year 2024 onwards
- **Trigger**: Unearned income exceeding €5,000 in a calendar year
- **Rate**: 4% on excess over €5,000 (Class K contribution)
- **Application**: Self-assessed via Form 11

### Unearned Income Includes
- Interest (bank deposits, bonds, savings accounts)
- Dividend income
- Rental income (net)
- Investment gains (CGT, exit tax gains)
- Royalties, patent income

### Critical Exclusion
- **PRSI 4% does NOT apply to exit tax gains** from deemed disposal or actual disposal of funds
- Exit tax is treated as a direct tax (like CGT) and is exempt from Class K PRSI
- Example: €10,000 exit tax bill in 2026 → No additional 4% PRSI charge; only the 41% exit tax is owed

### Interaction with €5,000 Self-Assessment Threshold
- Both PRSI (4%) and self-assessment (Form 11 filing) trigger at €5,000 non-PAYE income
- Triggering one effectively triggers the other
- File one Form 11 capturing both PRSI and income tax obligations

## Investment Platform Tax Considerations

### Tax Withholding & Reporting
- **Brokers (Trading 212, DeGiro, Revolut)**: No automatic withholding on UK gains; self-report via Form 11
- **Dividend Withholding**: UK shares typically report dividends net of UK withholding tax; include gross amount on Form 11
- **Interest (Revolut savings)**: Revolut reports interest to Revenue; DIRT automatically retained (33%) unless exemption claimed
- **Fund Platforms (Irish fund houses)**: Exit tax applied directly by fund manager; reported on Form 11

### Record-Keeping Requirements
- Maintain records for **6 years**: purchase invoices, sale confirmations, cost basis documentation
- Digital brokerage statements sufficient (PDF downloads)
- For fund holdings: annual fund statements showing cost basis
- Failure to produce records: Revenue can assess tax at their discretion + penalties

## Compliance Calendar (Tax Year)

| Event | Deadline | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Gains realized (Jan–Nov) | 15 December (same year) | File CG1/Form 11 |
| Gains realized (December) | 31 January (next year) | File CG1/Form 11 |
| Deemed disposal accrual | 8-year anniversary | Self-assess 41% exit tax on Form 11 |
| Annual tax return (Form 11) | 31 October (following year) | Submit via ROS with all reliefs, gains, PRSI |
| PAYE employee relief (Form 12) | 15 April (following year) | If claiming relief after year-end |
| Revenue audit window | 4 years | Records must be retained for audit inspection |

## Key Takeaways for Dublin Investors

1. **Exit tax (41%) on EU/Irish funds is unavoidable and automatic every 8 years** – Plan cash reserves for deemed disposal liability
2. **Deemed disposal triggers self-assessment** – File Form 11 even without selling; failure to do so incurs penalties
3. **PRSI 4% adds to tax burden** – Class K applies to investment income >€5,000; NOT to exit tax gains themselves
4. **US ETF restriction is firm** – As retail investor, focus on Irish/EU-domiciled UCITS ETFs; consider PRSA for tax-free growth
5. **Pension contributions deliver instant tax relief** – Marginal rate relief (20–40%) makes PRSA attractive vs. taxable accounts
6. **Record-keeping is critical** – 6-year retention required; Revenue can estimate if records unavailable
