Executive Summary

Paylane Technologies is building the payments infrastructure layer for Irish and UK SMEs that the incumbents have left underserved. Their core insight — that Irish VAT reconciliation, Revenue Commissioners API integration, and real-time FX hedging at sub-€1,000 minimums are genuinely unsolved for the SME segment — is validated by 87 paying customers, €1.2M ARR, and 118% NRR. The founding team carries direct, relevant pedigree: the CEO built SME payments at Stripe; the CTO ran the FX settlement engine at Wise.

The business has two genuine moats: the Revenue Commissioners API pilot (first-to-market, regulatory lag of 18–24 months for competitors) and the bank distribution flywheel via AIB and Bank of Ireland SME networks. A PSD2-native architecture means EU expansion requires sales headcount, not re-engineering.

The committee's conditional stance reflects two blocking issues: a runway cliff that leaves the company exposed to bridge risk during a 6–9 month raise process, and a valuation premium of 50–80% above comparable Irish B2B SaaS Series A deals in 2024. Neither is fatal — but both require resolution at term sheet stage.
