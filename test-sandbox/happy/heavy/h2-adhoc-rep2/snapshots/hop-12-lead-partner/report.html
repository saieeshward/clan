Full rationale — 11-analyst synthesis

01 Market TAM EUR 2.4M

EU mid-market logistics growing 12%; buyers prize time-to-value, EU residency, integration depth. HubSpot leads mindshare (34%) but Zoho (21%) gains on price.

02 Pricing

Per-seat: Zoho 38, HubSpot 50, Salesforce 75. At 40 seats Zoho is cheapest at EUR 18,240/yr; Salesforce ruled out on cost.

03 Risk

Migration data quality is the top risk for either finalist (High). Zoho carries slightly higher maturity risk; HubSpot maturity risk Low.

04 GDPR

EU (Frankfurt) residency required. Zoho includes EU pinning; HubSpot bills it as an add-on. Both sign a DPA; 30-day subprocessor notice.

05 Integrations

Six required (NetSuite, Slack, Twilio, DocuSign, Outlook, Shopify). HubSpot native 5, Zoho 4 (NetSuite via connector). ~9-week effort either way.

06 Customer discovery

6 interviews; top need lead response <2h; 90% adoption target; 4 champions, 24 training hours. Cost-conscious ops lean Zoho; HubSpot wins on UI.

07 Competitive intel

Zoho strength = price + EU residency; HubSpot = marketing suite; Salesforce = enterprise depth. Zoho's export clause keeps switching cost low.

08 Finance

Zoho saves ~EUR 17,280 over 3 years (54,720 vs 72,000) with a 7-month payback. Finance leans Zoho.

09 Rollout

Three phases: 2026-07-01, 2026-09-15, 2026-11-01 — core CRM live before the October peak; champion program in place.

10 Procurement

Procurable in ~4 weeks on a 1-year term. Leverage: annual prepay, multi-year option, competing bids. Push 60-day opt-out + Y2 cap.

11 Legal

DPA + SCCs with EU pinning cover transfers; customer-data IP clear. Liability cap and IP indemnity are thin — negotiate 3× cap + IP indemnity before signing.

Decision

Proceed with Zoho CRM at EUR 18,240 year-1, on a 1-year term, contingent on the legal and procurement terms above being secured at signing: EU Frankfurt pinning, quarterly export clause, DPA, 60-day auto-renew opt-out, Y2 price-uplift cap, 3× liability cap, and an explicit IP indemnification clause. Begin migration cleanse in Phase 1 (from 2026-07-01) to retire the High migration risk well ahead of the October peak.
