Why Zoho over the alternatives

  • Cost & payback (finance, pricing). Cheapest credible option at EUR 18,240 year-1; EUR 17,280 saved over 3 years vs HubSpot with a 7-month payback. Salesforce is eliminated on cost (EUR 36,000/yr).
  • EU data residency (GDPR, legal). Zoho includes Frankfurt EU region pinning at no extra cost — HubSpot bills it as an add-on. Data stays EU-only, so no cross-border transfer mechanism is needed (SCCs as fallback). Both vendors sign a DPA.
  • Market fit (market research). The EU mid-market logistics buyer prioritises time-to-value, EU residency, and integration depth — Zoho hits all three, and is the share-gainer in this segment on price.
  • Integrations are tractable (integrations). Zoho covers 4 of 6 required connectors natively; NetSuite is bridged via a supported connector. Effort is ~9 weeks either way, so HubSpot's one extra native connector does not justify its premium.
  • Customer pull (discovery). Reps want sub-2h lead response with minimal clicks; cost-conscious ops lean Zoho. A 4-champion program and 24 training hours target 90% adoption.
  • Low lock-in hedge (competitive intel, procurement). Zoho's quarterly export clause keeps switching cost low — a direct hedge against its maturity risk — and is contractually feasible.
