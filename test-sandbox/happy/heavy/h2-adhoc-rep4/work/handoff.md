# Handoff notes

## 01-market-researcher
Market favours fast EU-resident go-lives. HubSpot leads mindshare but Zoho is gaining in mid-market on price. Next: 02-pricing-analyst should get hard per-seat pricing for all three vendors.

## 02-pricing-analyst
Zoho is cheapest at EUR18,240/yr for 40 seats vs HubSpot 24,000. Salesforce is out on cost. Next: 03-risk-analyst should risk-weight the two finalists (Zoho vs HubSpot).

## 03-risk-analyst
Migration quality is the top risk for either vendor. Zoho carries slightly higher maturity risk on advanced workflows. Next: GDPR review.

## 04-gdpr-reviewer
Zoho includes EU region pinning at no extra cost; HubSpot bills it as an add-on. Both sign a DPA. Next: assess integrations.

## 05-integrations-assessor
HubSpot has one more native connector; Zoho needs a NetSuite connector but it is supported. ~9 wk effort either way. Next: customer discovery.

## 06-customer-discovery
Reps want fast lead response and minimal clicks. Split preference: cost-conscious ops lean Zoho. Next: competitive intel.

## 07-competitive-intel
Zoho's quarterly export clause keeps switching cost low — a hedge against its maturity risk. Next: finance model.

## 08-finance-modeler
Zoho saves ~EUR17,280 over 3 years with a 7-month payback. Finance leans Zoho. Next: rollout plan.

## 09-rollout-planner
Three phases land core CRM before the October peak with phase-2 marketing on 2026-09-15. Next: lead partner assembles the recommendation.

## 10-procurement-reviewer
1-yr term, ~4wk procurement lead. Zoho's bundled EU pinning + quarterly export clause are contractable; negotiate a renewal-uplift cap. Bake-off leverage keeps pricing honest. Next: lead partner assembles the recommendation.

## 11-legal-reviewer
DPA required and signable; EU pinning avoids transfer (SCCs as backstop). Liability cap = 1yr fees (EUR18,240), IP/indemnification adequate. Negotiate renewal-uplift cap. Next: lead partner assembles recommendation.
