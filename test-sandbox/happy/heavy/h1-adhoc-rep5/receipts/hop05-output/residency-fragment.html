Data residency

All Brightline customer and pipeline data will be stored in the vendor's EU (Frankfurt) region, with no replication outside the EEA. Zoho CRM provides region pinning on the Professional tier at no additional cost, and its standard Data Processing Agreement covers Brightline's GDPR obligations as data controller. Backups are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and retained for 30 days within the same region. For the NetSuite and Shopify integrations, only non-personal order metadata crosses the boundary, and that flow is documented in the integration register. A quarterly data-export clause guarantees Brightline can retrieve a full copy in a portable format, limiting lock-in. Sub-processors are listed in the vendor's trust centre and change-notified 30 days in advance, satisfying the COO's audit requirement before the October go-live.
