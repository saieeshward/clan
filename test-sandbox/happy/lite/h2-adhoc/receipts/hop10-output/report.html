Rationale

  • Cost leadership. Zoho delivers a EUR 17,280 saving over three years versus HubSpot — the next-cheapest finalist — with a 7-month payback. Salesforce was eliminated on cost alone.
  • GDPR compliance included. EU Frankfurt region pinning is bundled at no extra charge, satisfying Brightline's data-residency requirement without add-on negotiation.
  • Integration coverage is adequate. Four of six required connectors are native; the NetSuite gap is bridged by a supported third-party connector within the 9-week integration budget — the same effort required for HubSpot.
  • Low lock-in. Zoho's quarterly-export clause keeps switching cost low, providing a hedge against its medium maturity risk on advanced workflows.
  • Rollout fits the peak-season window. A three-phase plan lands core CRM by 2026-07-01, marketing automation by 2026-09-15, and full analytics by 2026-11-01 — giving the team a month of live operation before October peak.
  • Customer preference aligned. Cost-conscious operations stakeholders lean Zoho; UI-preference for HubSpot is addressable through the 24-hour champion training programme and a 4-person internal champion cohort.
