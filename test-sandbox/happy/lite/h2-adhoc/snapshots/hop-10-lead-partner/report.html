Why Zoho CRM

  • Lowest cost of the shortlist. EUR 18,240/yr vs HubSpot EUR 24,000 and Salesforce EUR 36,000 — saving ~EUR 17,280 over three years with a 7-month payback.
  • EU data residency included. Zoho pins data to the EU (Frankfurt) at no extra cost; HubSpot charges this as an add-on. Both vendors sign a DPA.
  • Meets the rollout deadline. A three-phase plan (1 Jul → 15 Sep → 1 Nov 2026) lands core CRM before the October peak, within ~9 weeks of effort either way.
  • Low switching cost as a hedge. Zoho's quarterly data-export clause keeps lock-in low, offsetting its higher maturity risk on advanced workflows.
  • Fits the buyer priorities. The market and customer interviews prize time-to-value, EU residency and fast lead response (<2h) — all areas where Zoho is competitive.

Rationale

Across nine independent analyses the signal is consistent: Zoho CRM is the cheapest credible option, delivers EU data residency without surcharge, and can be live before peak season. Its main weakness — maturity on advanced workflows — is structurally hedged by a low switching cost and a contractual quarterly export clause. HubSpot remains the stronger UI/marketing platform and a viable runner-up, but at a EUR 17,280 three-year premium that the buyer's priorities do not justify. Salesforce is excluded on cost.
