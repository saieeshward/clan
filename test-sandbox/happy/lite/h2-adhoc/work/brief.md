# Task: CRM Platform Recommendation for Brightline Logistics

Brightline Logistics (40 seats, EU-based, peak season starts October) needs a CRM
recommendation. A chain of specialist analysts will each add their findings, then a
lead partner assembles the final recommendation.

**Shortlist:** HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce.

**Each specialist:** add your bounded findings (≤15 fields) as structured data under
keys namespaced to your role (e.g. `pricing_*`, `risk_*`), and leave a short handoff
note (≤10 lines) for the next analyst. Do NOT re-transcribe prior analysts' findings —
build on them. The lead partner (final hop) produces the recommendation view.

**Decision to reach:** which vendor, at what year-1 cost, with what top risks.
