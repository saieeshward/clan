Competitive Landscape

Top 4 incumbents
DHL Supply Chain 18% share

Brand dominance, dense last-mile infrastructure, national coverage. Hard to displace on volume; vulnerable on SME agility and pricing flexibility.

DB Schenker 14% share

Rail integration and pan-EU reach. Strong for heavy industrial clients; weaker in e-commerce fulfillment and SME mid-market.

Rhenus Logistics 9% share

Mid-market regional hub operator. Closest proxy to Brightline's target positioning — signals viable niche, but direct competitive overlap likely.

Kühne+Nagel 7% share

Forwarding and pharma cold chain specialist. Limited overlap with Brightline's target segments; competitive pressure mainly in premium tiers.
