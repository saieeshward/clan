Aurora Dairy · Board Presentation · 10 July 2026 · Confidential

Velvet Oat:
Creamy Runs in the Family

The launch campaign recommendation — one idea, EUR 120,000, and a plan to make Irish oats the obvious choice in Irish coffee.

01 · Executive Summary

The creamiest country in Europe should not be losing its flat whites to imported oats.

Oat is Ireland's fastest-growing milk, and today it is owned by brands whose oats travel 2,400km to reach a Dublin chiller. Aurora Dairy holds the winning hand: three generations of creaminess credentials, 1,400 farming families, oats grown within 80km of your Cahir plant, and a product that already wins blind taste tests on creaminess and foam stability.

We recommend launching behind a single campaign — "Creamy Runs in the Family" — strengthened with the best of both alternative routes: the 80km provenance engine in out-of-home, and a dry, distinctly Irish voice in social. For EUR 120,000 it is built to deliver 12% of the oat segment, EUR 2.4m in retail sales and 150 cafes pouring Velvet Oat as their house oat within twelve months of the 7 September launch. Approve the route today and we are in production within a week.

02 · The Strategic Story

Your origin is not the liability. It is the entire idea.

Some will ask whether a dairy co-operative should make oat milk. Our audience has already answered: they keep both in the fridge. Irish people didn't abandon dairy — they fell in love with coffee, and oat milk is simply what great coffee drinks now. So who would you trust to make the creamiest oat milk in Ireland? The people who know creamy best.

Velvet Oat: the creamiest oat milk in Ireland, made by the people who know creamy best.

The campaign dramatises mastery handed down — from the churn to the portafilter. It is warm, cinematic and quietly proud: the structural opposite of the category's shouty tone, and the only route that delivers both halves of that proposition at once.

Creamy runs in the family. Tá an uachtar sa dúchas againn.*

*The Irish-language line is a working draft; it will be verified by a native speaker and TG4 copy review before production — scheduled and costed.

03 · The Campaign, Brought to Life

From a 1960s churn to a Dublin rosetta in thirty seconds.

The hero film

Archival-textured footage of butter being churned match-cuts through three generations of one Aurora family farm and lands on the Cahir line filling Velvet Oat cartons — then a barista stretching it into perfect microfoam. "We've spent three generations getting creamy right. The crop changed. The standard didn't." Cut to 15s and 6s for paid social.

Out-of-home, with the 80km engine

Farm-hand and barista-hand imagery on Dublin, Cork and Galway commuter corridors — and digital transit panels that compute the real distance from each panel to the growing fields. "These oats grew 63km from this bus stop." Provenance you can measure, on every execution.

Social and digital video

'Generations' films with real Aurora farming families, macro foam-stretch reels, and community management with a dry Irish wit borrowed from our third route — confident, never smug.

Cafes: the proof layer

A 150-cafe house-oat programme: cup sleeves, A-frames, side-by-side foam-stability demos and farmer-and-barista sampling days. Barista performance demonstrated, not claimed — exactly as the brief mandates.

04 · The Routes We Considered

Three strong routes. One clear winner — that borrows from the other two.

Route scorecard from concept development: fit to proposition, mandatories coverage, tonal risk and production fit.

"The 80km Flat White" turns your genuinely short supply chain into a mappable brag at every touchpoint. Brilliant — but it leads with provenance when your audience buys on taste and texture first, and comparative air-miles claims carry ASAI substantiation risk as a lead message. We keep its geo-distance engine as the campaign's provenance layer.

"Trust the Milk People" answers the bandwagon question with deadpan confidence. The cheapest to produce and the most shareable, but the highest tonal risk: irony can tip into exactly the knowing register our audience resents. We keep its voice for social community management.
