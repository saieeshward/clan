Brightline CRM Evaluation — Final Recommendation

Lead Partner synthesis · 10 June 2026 · Inputs: market-researcher, customer-discovery, risk-analyst · Flow: flow-07-parallel-adhoc-guided

Executive Summary

Recommendation: HubSpot (Sales Hub Professional + Marketing Hub Professional, EU/Frankfurt data hosting), at an estimated year-1 total cost of ~EUR 48,000 including licences, mandatory onboarding, training, migration, and a cost-creep contingency.

All three specialist work-streams independently converged on HubSpot, each at 4/5 confidence. For a 40-person Dublin ad agency dominated by account managers, media buyers, and creatives — not sales-ops engineers — HubSpot is the only candidate that simultaneously offers the lowest adoption risk (3–7 days to working competence, free role-based Academy training, no admin headcount), the only genuinely native Meta/Google/LinkedIn ads integration with ad-attributed revenue reporting, and a mid-pack total cost of ownership. Salesforce delivers more platform depth than Brightline can absorb at roughly twice the cost plus an admin role the agency does not have; Zoho is the budget fallback but adds integration assembly, weaker support, and thinner GDPR compliance documentation that an agency without a DPO would have to compensate for itself.

How the Specialists Were Weighed

Because the three roles agreed on the vendor, my reconciliation work was on cost and risk weighting, where they did diverge:

  • Cost range (market-researcher) vs cost-creep warning (risk-analyst). The market researcher's licence estimate spanned EUR 30–48k/yr depending on seat mix. The risk analyst flagged HubSpot's marketing-contact tiers and Hub bundling as a step-change cost risk. I resolved this by costing a concrete mixed-seat configuration (20 paid Sales Pro seats, 20 free core/read-only seats per the customer-discovery role mix of ~14 creatives + ~6 ops) and adding a 10% contingency line specifically for contact-tier creep, landing deliberately in the upper-middle of the researcher's range rather than at its optimistic floor.
  • GDPR: Salesforce "low" residual risk vs HubSpot "low-medium" (risk-analyst). Salesforce technically has the strongest formal compliance posture. I judged that the marginal GDPR delta does not outweigh Salesforce's severity-4 adoption and lock-in risks for a 40-seat firm; HubSpot's residual exposure is fully addressable with three cheap mitigations adopted into the rollout plan: select EU (Frankfurt) hosting at portal creation (irreversible later — the risk register's severity-4 item), a written transfer impact assessment for US subprocessors, and quarterly full data exports as an exit hedge.
  • Training cost. Customer-discovery's EUR 2–5k HubSpot training estimate is folded in at EUR 3,500 (midpoint), versus EUR 15–30k it projected for Salesforce — a gap that alone neutralises much of Zoho's headline licence saving once Zoho's EUR 5–12k training/champion burden is counted.

Three-Phase Rollout Plan

Phase 1 — Foundation & Pipeline (Weeks 1–4)

Create the portal with EU (Frankfurt) hosting verified before any data import; execute DPA and transfer impact assessment; appoint two champions (senior AM + media buyer) with weekly office hours; migrate live clients and last 12 months of history only; onboard the ~12 AMs on pipelines, email auto-logging, and the three non-negotiable behaviours modelled by leadership.

Phase 2 — Ad-Ops Integration (Weeks 5–8)

Onboard the ~8 media buyers; connect Meta, Google, and LinkedIn ad accounts via the native Ads tool; wire lead-form capture and ad-attributed revenue reporting; add GA4 via standard connectors. Adoption checkpoint at week 8: <70% AM weekly-active triggers intervention.

Phase 3 — Full Rollout & Hardening (Weeks 9–12)

Creatives and remaining staff on free read-only seats (no mandatory workflows); decommission side spreadsheets; schedule quarterly full data exports to Brightline-controlled storage; review contact-tier consumption and confirm renewal pricing; week-12 adoption metrics review and sign-off.

Caveat: all pricing and vendor capabilities are knowledge-based (early-2026 cutoff), compiled without web access. Verify HubSpot list pricing, EU hosting terms, and DPF status against current vendor documentation before contract signature.
