Honest headline: at 3–4 hops CLAN does not win raw tokens — disciplined ad-hoc is 15–40% leaner, because CLAN's context carries schema, chain, and guide scaffolding. What offsets it: --skip-guide saves ~1.86k tok/hop branch agents read ~2.5k chars, never a sibling transcript merge costs zero LLM tokens. What the overhead buys is the rest of this report.

The cleanest result in the benchmark: removing prompt guidance cost ad-hoc flows their structured data, provenance, and human view — and cost CLAN flows nothing. The format, not the prompt, carries the protocol.

Concurrency & conflicts

All three parallel branches independently wrote an assumptions key. Last-write silently preferred customer-discovery's version — dropping the risk-analyst's GDPR/PII scoping — and merge-report.yaml caught exactly that, with winner/loser provenance; the synthesizer restored both framings and recorded the adjudication. The only arm in the benchmark where a silent information loss was detected and repaired. Ad-hoc parallel flows avoided collisions purely by emergent convention (role-prefixed filenames every agent happened to choose).

Reliability & the teachable interface

30/30 agents completed; 3 errors, all self-recovered (one real CLAN DX bug: UTF-8 BOM rejection — F3). Unguided CLAN agents reached full protocol competence from agent-help + hints alone at ~2–5k discovery tokens; one synthesizer discovered clan merge unprompted and verified data survival afterwards. The namespace guard was never even triggered. Hints helped in 4 receipts and misled in 1 scenario (stale-view → render on a hand-authored view — F2).

Verdict: CLAN's price is a modest, bounded token overhead. Its product is that correctness, provenance, conflict detection, and human attribution survive even when nobody writes a careful prompt — which is exactly the regime real multi-framework pipelines live in.

Method: 30 real subagents (no scripted outputs), identical brief, knowledge-only; context sizes measured from hop snapshots; receipts written by the agents themselves. Full narrative: research/14-flow-benchmark.md. Total spend 967,683 subagent tokens; flows 1–8 ran concurrently in 17.6 minutes.
